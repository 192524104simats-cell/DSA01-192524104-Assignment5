# B1 – Billing System Test Results

## Compilation
Command:
g++ -std=c++17 -Wall -Wextra Task06/B1_Billing_System.cpp -o Task06/B1_Billing_System.exe

Result: Successful compilation with no warnings.

## Test Cases

| Bill Type | Bill Amount | SelfPay | Insurance (80%) | Government |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| OutPatient | 500 | 500 | 400 | 400 |
| InPatient | 2000 | 2000 | 1600 | 1500 |
| DayCare | 1000 | 1000 | 800 | 800 |
| Emergency | 3000 | 3000 | 2400 | 2500 |

## Observation
- SelfPay covers the complete bill.
- Insurance covers 80% of the bill.
- GovernmentScheme covers fixed amounts based on bill type.
- All 12 bill-payer combinations produced the expected results.

## Conclusion
Runtime polymorphism successfully calculates the amount covered by each payer for all four bill types.
